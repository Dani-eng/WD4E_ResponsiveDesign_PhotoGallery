British Columbia is the westernmost province of Canada with a total area of approximately 945,000 square kilometers. It is the third largest pronvice and is situated between the Rocky Mountains on the east and the Pacific Ocean on the west.

Over three-quarters of the province is mountinous due to the presence of major mountain ranges like the Coast Mountains and the Canadian Rockies. The land also ranges from temperate coastal rainforests and rugged islands (like those on Vancouver Island) to semi-arid interior regions like the Okanagan Valley, which is home to its famous vineyards. Additionally, the Port of Vancouver is Canada's largest port.
